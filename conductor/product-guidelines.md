# Product Guidelines

## Voice and Tone
Professional, technical, direct, and unambiguous.

## Design Principles
- Simplicity over complexity.
- Performance and context efficiency first.
- Zero simulation and verified live data invariant (anti-mock).

## Operational Standards
- The agent must operate systematically and strictly adhere to deterministic boundaries (depth, count, language).
- Every research task must use a dedicated directory, isolated SQLite database, and centralized metrics registration.
